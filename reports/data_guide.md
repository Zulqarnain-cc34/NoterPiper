## 1. Prayers — Namaz & Jamaat

I track my **five daily prayers**, one habit per prayer, every tap **timestamped**.
For each prayer I record **where** I prayed it with a single tap:

| Tap | What it means |
|:--:|---|
| ➕ | I prayed it **in Jama** (in congregation, at the mosque) |
| ➖ | I prayed it **at home / alone** (not in Jama) |
| *(no tap)* | I **missed** it |

Tap after each prayer, or tap all five at night — either way it's saved.

### The five prayers
| | Prayer | Time of day |
|:--:|---|---|
| 🌅 | **Fajr** | dawn, before sunrise |
| ☀️ | **Zohar** | midday, after noon |
| 🌤️ | **Asr** | afternoon |
| 🌇 | **Maghrib** | just after sunset |
| 🌙 | **Isha** | night |

*(Each is a ➕ / ➖ habit — ➕ in Jama, ➖ at home, no tap = missed.)*

### What this tells me
Every prayer, every day, falls into **one of three categories** — tracked
**individually for each of the five prayers**:

| | Category | How it's logged |
|:--:|---|---|
| 🟢 | **In Jamaat** — prayed in congregation | ➕ |
| 🔵 | **Prayed** — at home / alone | ➖ |
| 🔴 | **Missed** — not prayed | *(no tap)* |

So for **each prayer** (Fajr, Zohar, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) I can see over time how
often it was **in Jamaat**, **prayed at home**, or **missed** — and at what time.

### Fixing mistakes
- Tapped the wrong one? Tap the right one — **last tap of the day wins** (or *Adjust Counter*).
- Worried you forgot? Each night, glance at the five and tap any you prayed.

## 2. Daily Recitations

Things I recite **every day**. Each one is a **simple Daily** — **one tap = done
for today.**

| | Recitation | What it is |
|:--:|---|---|
| 📖 | **Surah Mulk** | Recite Surah Mulk (for the afterlife) |
| 📿 | **500 Lā ḥawla** | Say *Lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh* ×500 (wazifa) |
| 📿 | **100 Tasbeeh Fatima** | The Tasbeeh Fatima dhikr |
| 💚 | **11 Times Muhammad** | Say *Muhammad* ×11 (focus on the heart) |
| 📖 | **Quran Hakeem** | Read at least 3 big surahs at Fajr |

### How it works
- **Tap when done** → that day is recorded (timestamped).
- **Didn't tap** → the Daily shows undone and nudges you — so you notice a miss.
- **Streak** = your "on a roll" motivation (it resets if you miss — that's fine).

### What this tells me
For each recitation, over time: **how many days done, how many missed, current
and longest streak, and how steady I've been.** The count comes from the taps.

## 3. Weekly Recitations (Fridays)

Recited only on **Friday (Jummah)**. Each is a **Daily** set to **Weekly → Friday**,
so Habitica only makes it due on Fridays — a quiet Mon–Thu is never a miss.

| | Recitation | What it is |
|:--:|---|---|
| 📿 | **313 Darood** | Darood Sharif ×313 on Friday |
| 📖 | **Quran Every Jummah** | Read Surah Dukhan on Jummah |

### How it works
- **Due only on Fridays** → tap when done.
- Other days it isn't due, so it won't nudge you or count as missed.
- **Streak** = Fridays in a row.

### What this tells me
How many **Fridays done vs missed**, and the Friday streak over time.
